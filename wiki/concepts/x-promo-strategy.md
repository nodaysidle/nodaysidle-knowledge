# X promo strategy

## Summary

All posts go out first-person from Adlof's personal X account, which has X Premium and some reach but few followers. Each post sells one problem and its result and links one specific repo or release. Every post gets a visual. REACH drafts and Adlof posts; nothing is posted or scheduled by an agent.

## Details

### Account decision (2026-10-06)

- Post from the **personal account**. A new brand account would start at near-zero reach, and reposts carry less weight than originals.
- Bio: "building @nodaysidle apps" plus the portfolio link. Pin the Kurek launch post.
- Reserve the `nodaysidle` handle and keep it quiet. Revisit a brand account at ~1–2k followers or when one app takes off.

### Positioning

"One dev, a lot of shipped apps." There are 36 public repos, and every post points to something you can download.

### Post types (rotate)

| Type | Shape |
|------|-------|
| Launch | one app, one problem → result, one link, GIF or MP4 |
| Thread | how it was built or what was learned, link in the last post |
| Weekly roundup | Friday evening, apps of the week plus portfolio link in a reply |
| Build in public | no link: work-in-progress screenshots, bugs, numbers |

### Copy rules

- Open with the problem, then "I built / I shipped X", then the link.
- Put the link in the post body for launches. For the roundup, put it in the first reply.
- Hashtags: at most 2. `#buildinpublic` plus one platform tag (`#macapps`, `#linux`, `#rustlang`, `#androiddev`).
- Copy may only claim what the clip actually shows and what the repo supports (see caveats in [[wiki/concepts/x-promo-calendar-2026-10]]).
- Licences: kureksistant is CC BY-NC 4.0 and derived from FatihMakes' JARVIS, so credit it. ShareGuard is proprietary, so say "DMG on GitHub", never "MIT". Don't promote the 12 private repos.

### Timing (Europe/Ljubljana)

- Launches: **Tue–Thu ~15:15** (EU afternoon plus US-East morning, 9:15 New York).
- Friday roundup: **~18:15**.
- After the EU clock change (25 Oct), keep the same local times. Until the US changes (1 Nov), New York is 5h behind, so 15:15 here is 10:15 there.
- Avoid Monday mornings and weekends for launches.

### Daily habits

- Reply to every comment in the first 30–60 min after posting.
- Post 5–10 thoughtful replies a day to larger indie, Mac, Linux, or dev accounts. Premium replies rank high and send people to the profile and pinned post.
- Between launches: 1–2 posts without links. X ranks posts without external links higher.

### Evidence used

- Research on comparable indie posts (2026-10-06): a video-demo app launch reached ~358k impressions, while text-only DMG drops stayed around 200–500. First-person framing plus media performed best. Worldwide trends were not useful (mostly sports).
