# X promo strategy from Mon 12 Oct 2026 (with week-1 drafts)

## Summary

This plan revises the week of 12 Oct in [[wiki/concepts/x-promo-calendar-2026-10]] to fit the nodaysrammar v1.0.1 release (9 Oct) and the downloads showcase (https://nodaysidle-apps.vercel.app). The goal is clicks and recognition for github.com/nodaysidle, the portfolio and the showcase. Adlof posts manually from his personal account. REACH drafts only and never posts, schedules or sends. The base rules (account, positioning, copy rules, licences) stay in [[wiki/concepts/x-promo-strategy]].

## Cadence

- **Per day:** at most one launch (a post with a link) and at most one post without a link. Never two launches on the same day; the Friday roundup is the exception.
- **Per week:** 4 launches Tue–Fri (one of them a thread), 1 Friday roundup with links only in replies, 2–3 build-in-public posts without links, and 35–70 replies to other accounts.
- **Weekend:** at most one post without a link (a poll or numbers), then replies only. Send REACH the 24h numbers on Sunday for the Monday 09:17 scan.

## Times (Europe/Ljubljana)

| Slot | Local | New York until 24 Oct (EDT, −6h) | 25 Oct–1 Nov (−5h) | From 2 Nov (EST, −6h) |
|------|-------|-----------------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| Launch, Tue–Fri | 15:15 | 09:15 | 10:15 | 09:15 |
| Friday roundup | 18:15 | 12:15 | 13:15 | 12:15 |
| Weekday evening post, no link | 20:30 | 14:30 | 15:30 | 14:30 |
| Monday or weekend post, no link | 15:15 Mon / 17:00 Sat | 09:15 / 11:00 | 10:15 / 12:00 | 09:15 / 11:00 |

- **Why 15:15:** it hits the EU afternoon and US-East as people sit down to work, which is the largest overlap of the dev audience. The US West Coast catches up later in the day, inside the first ~24h of the post.
- **Why 18:15 Friday:** EU people are logging off and US-East is at lunch, a good moment for weekend browsing of a "what I shipped" roundup.
- **Why 20:30 for posts without links:** EU evening plus US afternoon, and it doesn't compete with the 15:15 launch.
- **Clock changes:** Europe goes from CEST to CET on Sun 25 Oct and the US changes on Sun 1 Nov. Keep the same local times throughout. Only the New York offset changes, as shown in the table.
- Avoid launches on Monday mornings and weekends.

## Content mix

| Type | Share | Shape |
|------|-------|-------|
| Launch | ~45% | problem → "so I built X" → what it does (only verified claims) → one release link → MP4 attached. First reply links the showcase card (`#anchor`) or the portfolio. |
| Thread | 1/week | how it's built or what I learned; the link only in the last post; clip on post 3 |
| Roundup | 1/week, Fri | no link in the main post; reply 1 = showcase, reply 2 = portfolio + github.com/nodaysidle |
| Build in public (no link) | ~35% | screenshots, numbers, honest fixes, polls. X ranks posts without external links higher. |

Hashtags: at most 2. Always `#buildinpublic`, plus `#opensource` only for MIT/OSS repos (nodaysrammar, Sonora, Cascade, Synapse Notes), `#rustlang`, `#linux`, `#macapps` or `#androiddev`. Never use `#opensource` for ShareGuard (proprietary), ClipRail or Browser for Linux (no licence file).

## Catalog rotation

1. The newest release goes first in the week (Tue).
2. Apps on the showcase (Cascade, Sonora, WhisperBar, Browser for Linux, nodaysrammar) rotate first, so that every launch also sends clicks to the showcase.
3. No app is the headline more than once per 14 days. Roundups and threads can mention it again.
4. Then the rest of the public catalog: Kurek (kureksistant), Synapse Notes, ClipRail, ShareGuard. Check that each one has a downloadable release before its slot.
5. Never promote private repos (including kurekizmo).

## Daily reply habit

- 15 min before the 15:15 launch: 5 thoughtful replies to bigger indie, Linux/Hyprland, Mac, Rust, Chrome-extension or privacy accounts. No links in those replies.
- 0–60 min after the launch: answer every reply to your own post.
- Evening: 3–5 more replies. The target is 5–10 a day. With Premium these replies rank high and send people to the profile and pinned post.
- Pin the week's best-performing launch. Bio: "building @nodaysidle apps" + the showcase link.
